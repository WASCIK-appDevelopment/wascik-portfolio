# WASCIK Growth Engine — Keyword Opportunity Brief

Date: August 13, 2026  
Status: Baseline before Google Search Console data

## Recommendation

WASCIK should first own its brand searches and compete for **affordable small-business website development in Little Rock and Central Arkansas**. The existing `/start-project` route should become the primary commercial search page. We should not begin by producing many separate city pages.

The Affiliate Services homepage is the second indexing priority. Its first product experiment should remain the portable voice-recorder guide, but that page is a lower-probability search opportunity because broad recorder results are dominated by major retailers and established review publishers.

## What the search landscape shows

- Local web-design results are crowded with established agencies and directories. Common messages include Little Rock location, affordability, mobile-friendly design, SEO, quotes, and local support.
- WASCIK can differentiate with a concrete $324 promotional offer, direct founder access, transparent ongoing costs, mobile-first work, and plain-English support.
- Small-business AI searches are dominated by national publishers. Arkansas-specific competition appears thinner, but WASCIK should not lead with that topic until its AI assistant demonstration and offer are ready.
- Broad recorder searches are highly competitive. Original testing, exact product identification, benefits, limitations, and honest use cases are necessary to give the guide a meaningful reason to rank.

## Priority order

1. **P0 — WASCIK App Development:** secure brand indexing and consistent company information.
2. **P0 — Affordable website developer in Little Rock:** strengthen `/start-project` around the verified offer and buying questions.
3. **P0 — Small-business website development in Arkansas:** support the same page with natural Arkansas relevance.
4. **P0 — WASCIK Affiliate Services:** improve trust, selection standards, author identity, and guide navigation.
5. **P1 — Website maintenance in Arkansas:** add a substantial maintenance section using verified pricing.
6. **P1 — Church website development:** wait for an approved project or case study before creating a focused page.
7. **P2 — AI website assistants:** build proof before creating an acquisition page.
8. **P2 — Portable voice recorder:** improve after exact model and specifications are verified.

## Measurement caveat

The scores in `keyword-opportunity-map.json` are directional judgments based on current search-result competition, business value, relevance, attainability, and page readiness. Recalculate priorities after deployment and 30–60 days of impression data.
